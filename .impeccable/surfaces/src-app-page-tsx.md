# Surface: portfolio home (src/app/page.tsx)

Scope: the single-page portfolio. Visitor mode: Experience (portfolio), with Persuade duties: email, resume and GitHub/LinkedIn always one move away.
Audience: remote/international and PH tech recruiters and engineering leads, skimming on laptop or phone.
Must keep: magnetic/morphing buttons and avatar, coverflow project carousel, frosted glass, scroll progress, floating orbs, dot grid, button shine sweeps, theme toggle (dark-first), mobile nav. Recommendations become an auto-advancing quote carousel with full text available. Hero carries a scripted AI chat answered only from resume facts, labeled as a scripted demo.
Red lines: lag on mid-range phones, gimmicky/cringe flash, generic blue-purple AI-portfolio look.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Full-stack as light through layers. One white beam enters Sean's glass prism and leaves as a working spectrum, one band per layer he ships. Refuses the centered-avatar, gradient-text, purple-glass dev portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: Black optical bench (#07080d) drilled with a breadboard hole grid; cut-glass panels whose edges disperse into spectrum where the cursor touches; one white beam; a six-band spectrum (UI red-orange, API amber, DATA green, AI cyan, CLOUD blue, LAB violet) that encodes skill layers everywhere. Expanded grotesk display (Archivo wdth 125), Geist body, Geist Mono only for readouts and data.

STORY: The visitor sees the claim (full-stack + AI builder), watches the beam split into the layers he works in, asks the chat a question, then follows the spectrum through skills, experience, education, projects and recommendations, and leaves through email, resume or GitHub.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Nav top. Name set huge across two lines at top-left with the one-line claim and location beside line two. Below, left: a glass prism holding the magnetic photo; a beam enters from the viewport's left edge and its incidence follows the cursor (live θ readout); the spectrum fans right into the chat panel, labeled UI/API/DATA/AI/CLOUD/LAB at its edge. CTAs (Get in Touch primary, GitHub, LinkedIn) under the prism. Mobile stacks name, claim, CTAs, prism, chat, spectrum fanning down.

FORM: Prism Bench, pick card (my rank 1 of 7) chosen by the user over the rolled Chrome Rookie Card; seed key 8d6de8ca.

Signature interaction: cursor steers the beam's angle of incidence and the spectrum swings. Motion grammar: light reveals (clip/mask wipes in the direction of travel), exponential ease-out, everything visible by default, reduced-motion renders the static split.
